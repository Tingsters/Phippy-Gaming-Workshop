## Source records and changes

Retrieved on **2026-10-09** from the creators' websites or the original OpenGameArt upload. [sources.json](sources.json) records source pages, versions, download URLs, and SHA-256 hashes of the downloaded ZIPs. SunnyLand was downloaded using its free `Sunny-land-files.zip` option (upload ID `18679034`); the stable download page is recorded instead of an expiring download URL.

[pixel/manifest.json](pixel/manifest.json) maps every delivered PNG to its upstream path and records its size and SHA-256. Every delivered art PNG is an unchanged copy of its selected original; changes are descriptive filenames and category organization. Exact duplicate food/background images were omitted. The manifest records omitted files and reasons.

The contact sheets in `assets/previews/` are workshop-created arrangements of the credited artwork, enlarged with nearest-neighbor scaling. Their credits are printed on each sheet and linked from every category guide. The food contact sheets and overview include CC BY 3.0 artwork; preserve its credit when reusing those previews.

## Selection for the kids workshop

The selected images were visually reviewed as contact sheets. They contain colorful characters, animals, scenery, food, vehicles, symbols, and cartoon obstacles. No blood, gore, nudity, sexual imagery, or adult-themed artwork is included. Skull decorations and their combined sheets, bow/arrow items, military vehicle variants, and shooter weapon pieces were omitted. Cartoon bumps, falling poses, spikes, and magic poofs are included.

Only artwork and license notices were imported from the source packs. Music, engine demo projects, advertisements, platform metadata, and editor source files were not added.
